**What:**
- Added explicit `id` attributes to all tab buttons and linked them to their respective panels using `aria-labelledby`.
- Added `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-atomic="true"` to the dynamically-updated error badge.

**Why:**
- To ensure users relying on screen readers can properly track active tabs and recognize when the hidden error badge appears or updates dynamically.

**Before/After:**
- The tabs previously lacked structural association for assistive technologies, and the badge's dynamic behavior bypassed screen readers.
- Now, screen readers will explicitly relate panels to their tabs and proactively announce changes to the error count.

**Accessibility:**
- Improves semantic relationship between `tablist`, `tab`, and `tabpanel` components.
- Fixes dynamic notification missing for non-visual users using WAI-ARIA live regions.
