## 2024-03-20 - Missing Connective ARIA Attributes
**Learning:** Dynamic UI elements in this app (tabs, counters, validation errors) frequently omit connective ARIA attributes (`aria-labelledby`, `aria-live`, `role="alert"`), despite having structural ARIA roles like `role="tab"`. This creates an inconsistent screen reader experience where tabs are identified but not linked to their content, and dynamic updates (like error counts) happen silently.
**Action:** When creating or modifying dynamic components, always check for complete ARIA patterns (e.g., matching `id` and `aria-labelledby`, or adding `aria-live` to dynamically updated text).
